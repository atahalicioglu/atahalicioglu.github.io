Privacy Policy: Midnight Heist

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Midnight Heist ("the game") is a mobile puzzle game. This page says what the game does with information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, lives, settings) is saved on your device only. We run no server that holds it. Deleting the game deletes it.

What is collected

  • Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): events such as opening the app, starting or finishing a level, using a tool, and watching a rewarded ad, with a random install ID. No name, email, contact or location data.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): the game offers optional rewarded video ads. To show them, Google and its ad partners may use your device's advertising identifier and similar data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first. On iOS you can allow or refuse tracking in the system prompt; the game works the same either way.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when your lives are full). Nothing is sent from a server.

What is not collected

No account is needed. We do not ask for or store your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not sell personal data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Refuse or withdraw ad consent and tracking in the game's prompts and in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Turn off notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.

Services we use and their policies

  • Google (Firebase, AdMob): https://policies.google.com/privacy
